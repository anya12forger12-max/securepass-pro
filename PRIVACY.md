# Privacy Policy — SecurePass Pro

Last updated: September 2026

## 1. Introduction

SecurePass Pro ("we", "our", "us") is a local password and secret manager. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data and privacy.

## 2. Information We Collect

SecurePass Pro does not require an account and does not collect personal information from you. The passwords, notes, and secrets you store in the app are kept on your device in an encrypted vault.

No registration, email address, or phone number is required to use the application.

## 3. How Your Data Is Stored

- Your vault is encrypted and stored only on your device.
- The encryption key is derived from your master password using strong cryptographic algorithms.
- Your vault data is not transmitted to our servers.
- If you enable cloud backup, encrypted vault files are stored with the provider you select; backups are encrypted before upload.

## 4. Data Privacy & Security

- Your data belongs solely to you.
- We cannot read your vault, recover your master password, or access your stored secrets.
- Your secrets are never shared with other users.
- Industry-standard encryption protects your data at rest.

## 5. Your Rights

- Export or delete your vault at any time.
- Clear all app data from your device settings.
- Your on-device vault data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## 6. Advertising and Advertising IDs

SecurePass Pro is a free app supported by advertising served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier (Advertising ID) to display and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

- We do not sell your data to advertisers.
- You may see personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads > "Delete advertising ID") and in Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).
- Google's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
- Where required by law, a consent dialog is shown before personalized ads are served.

## 7. Children's Privacy

SecurePass Pro is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time by posting a new version on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
