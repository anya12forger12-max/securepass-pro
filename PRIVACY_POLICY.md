## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

SecurePass Pro does not require an account and does not collect personal information from you. The passwords, notes, and secrets you store in the app are kept on your device in an encrypted vault.

No registration, email address, or phone number is required to use the application.

---

## 2. HOW YOUR DATA IS STORED

- Your vault is encrypted and stored only on your device.
- The encryption key is generated on your device and kept in the device's secure storage. **There is no master password**, and the key is never transmitted anywhere.
- Your vault data is not transmitted to our servers. We operate no server that receives your vault or password contents.
- **There is no built-in cloud backup and the app never uploads anything.** When you export a backup, the app encrypts it and places the encrypted JSON envelope on your clipboard. If you paste that envelope into a third-party storage service you choose (for example a drive, email, or notes app), that service becomes the storage provider and applies its own access, retention, and metadata policies. SecurePass Pro itself never contacts or transmits to such a service.
- Exported backups are **device-bound**: the encryption key is not included in the backup file, so an envelope can only be restored on the device that created it. Restoring a backup onto a new device is not currently supported.

---

## 3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS REQUESTED

SecurePass Pro requests no runtime permissions. All vault operations are performed locally on your device without accessing the network.

---

## 4. DATA SECURITY

- **Encryption:** AES-256-GCM encryption protects your vault at rest. Each encryption operation uses a unique nonce generated by the platform's cryptographically secure random number generator.
- **Key Derivation:** PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 210,000 iterations and a per-user random salt secures your vault PIN.
- **Clipboard Safety:** Copied secrets are automatically cleared from the system clipboard after a configurable timeout. The clipboard deadline is persisted and enforced on every app launch.
- **No Remote Access:** We cannot read your vault, recover your master password, or access your stored secrets.

---

## 5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

* **Retention:** Your vault data exists only on your device for as long as the app is installed.
* **Deletion:** You may export or delete your vault at any time within the app. Clearing all app data from your device settings or uninstalling the app permanently erases your vault.

## 7. USER RIGHTS (INCLUDING GDPR / CCPA COMPLIANCE)

Because SecurePass Pro collects no personal data and operates no servers, most GDPR/CCPA rights are satisfied by design:
* **Right to Access:** Your data never leaves your device; you have full access at all times.
