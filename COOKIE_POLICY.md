# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-10-01
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website or use an application. They help the application remember your preferences and improve your experience.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)
These cookies are required for the application to function properly and cannot be disabled:
- **Authentication cookies**: Keep you signed in during your session
- **Security cookies**: Protect against CSRF and other attacks
- **Session cookies**: Maintain your session state

### 2.2 Preference Cookies
These cookies remember your preferences:
- **Theme preference**: Light/dark/system theme selection
- **Language preference**: Your selected language
- **Notification settings**: Your notification preferences

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies (Optional)
We do not currently use analytics cookies in this application. All usage data is stored locally on your device.

### 2.4 Advertising Cookies
We use Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may use cookies for:
- Personalized ad delivery (with your consent)
- Ad frequency capping
- Ad performance measurement

## 3. Cookie Consent

On your first visit, you will see a consent banner allowing you to:
- **Accept all cookies**: Enables all cookie categories
- **Reject non-essential cookies**: Only essential cookies will be used
- **Manage preferences**: Choose which cookie categories to enable

You can change your preferences at any time in Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences.

## 4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:
- **Application settings**: Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences
- **Browser settings**: Clear cookies via your browser's privacy settings
- **Device settings**: Android Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Storage → Clear Data

Note: Disabling essential cookies will prevent the application from functioning correctly.

## 5. Third-Party Cookies

| Provider | Purpose | Opt-Out Link |
|----------|---------|--------------|

## 6. Data Retention

- **Session cookies**: Deleted when you close the app
- **Persistent cookies**: Retained for up to 12 months or until you clear them
- **Authentication tokens**: Retained per Firebase Auth policy (typically 1 hour refresh, long-lived access tokens)

## 7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy laws, you have the right to:
- Access your cookie data
- Request deletion of cookie data
- Object to cookie processing
- Withdraw consent at any time
